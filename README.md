## ✨ Features

- ✍️ Edit everything inline: your details, client, invoice number, dates
- ➕ Unlimited line items with automatic totals
- 💱 USD, BDT, EUR, GBP, INR and CHF
- 🏷️ Discount and tax / VAT
- 🖼️ Add your logo
- 📄 One-click **Download PDF** (clean print layout)
- 💾 Auto-saves in your browser; **New invoice** keeps your details and bumps the number
- 📱 Works on mobile

## 🚀 Why I built it

Most invoice tools want an account or a subscription. Freelancers just need a good-looking invoice, fast.

## 🛠️ How to use

1. Open the **[live demo](https://aliakbarrme.github.io/quick-invoice/)**
2. Fill in your details, the client and the line items
3. Click **Download PDF** and choose *Save as PDF*

To run it locally, download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. No build step, no server.
